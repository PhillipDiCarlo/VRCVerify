{# ONE PRODUCT, THREE CADENCES, SAID OUTRIGHT. Three cards at rising prices is the shape of a tier comparison, and a reader who scans rather than reads will take the $47.99 card to unlock more than the $4.99 one. It does not: all three are the same Premium at a different billing period. Layout alone cannot fix that. The reference that solves it best (Uxcel) normalises both its cards to one unit -- "$20 per month" beside "$8 per month, paid yearly" -- which cannot be misread. That move is not available here: it needs a per-month figure derived from the charge, and this repo holds that no amount is ever computed, because a second copy of a price on a page about money is a second thing to be wrong. See the comment on .plan-saving. So the claim is stated in words instead, above the cards, before anyone starts comparing them. #}
