{#- THE MARK. One path, and the digits are a knockout. WHAT IT IS: the age-rating plaque the product has always used, redrawn for #288. The old one was a 1024x1024 PNG carrying "18+" over a second bar reading "VRCV", cropped and scaled down to draw a 22px mark -- at which size "VRCV" is a smudge saying a worse version of the word sitting next to it, and the two nested rounded rectangles have a sub-pixel gap between them. THE DIGITS ARE INTER, cut from the same static/fonts/inter-latin-var.woff2 the page is set in, at weight 800, positioned from the font's own metrics rather than by eye. So the mark and the wordmark beside it are the same typeface, which the old one never was. ONE PATH WITH fill-rule="evenodd", so the plaque is painted and the digits are the page showing through. That is why there is one colour here and not two: `currentColor` takes the accent from whichever stylesheet this lands in, the counters of the 8 fall back to plaque colour on their own, and the mark needs no second token plumbed through four theme positions in three files. It also survives being dropped on a coloured surface, where a mark with hard-coded white digits would not. A would do the same job and was rejected: masks need ids, ids must be unique in a document, and this mark appears twice on the sign-in page. aria-hidden because the wordmark beside it says the name. A screen reader announcing "VRCVerify logo, VRCVerify" is a worse link than one that just says where it goes. -#}
