18+ verification for Discord, using VRChat's own age check

A member links their VRChat account, VRCVerify reads whether VRChat has age-verified them, and the bot assigns the role. No documents are handled here, no photographs, and nothing about a member's identity is shown to anyone.

Add to your server Open the dashboard

Verification is free, and stays free

Every server can verify members, auto-verify people who have already verified elsewhere, and let members update their own nickname, at no cost. That is the part that matters and it is not behind a paywall.

A per-server subscription unlocks the automation around it: automatic removal of an unverified role, nickname syncing, a custom post-verification message, a verification log channel, priority in the verification queue, and a branded instructions panel. It can be bought through Discord monthly, or with a card on the dashboard where six- and twelve-month plans are also available.

What it does not do

  • It does not see, store or transmit identity documents. VRChat performs the age verification; VRCVerify reads the result.
  • It does not store your VRChat display name or your profile bio.
  • It does not handle card details. Payments go to Stripe or to Discord, and neither passes card data to us.
  • It gives server administrators no way to mark someone verified by hand. There is no override, deliberately.
