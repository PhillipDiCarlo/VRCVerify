1. What the service does

VRCVerify checks whether a member's VRChat account has been age verified by VRChat, and assigns a Discord role accordingly. It also provides related automation that a server administrator can configure.

We do not perform age verification ourselves. VRChat does. VRCVerify reads the result VRChat reports and acts on it. We do not handle identity documents, and we cannot vouch for the accuracy of VRChat's determination, the honesty of the person who made it, or whether the VRChat account belongs to the person using the Discord account beyond the code check described in the Privacy Policy.
